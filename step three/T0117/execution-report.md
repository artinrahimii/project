- **Final implementation/acceptance commit:** `V 3.0.354` — [`6653db2be37ba5b4ff964543efab997dd217aa9b`](https://github.com/artinrahimii/project/commit/6653db2be37ba5b4ff964543efab997dd217aa9b).
- **Library:** `Nethermind.Libp2p` `1.0.3`, MIT, .NET 10. Sources: [NuGet package](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Nethermind.Libp2p) and [project repository](https://github.com/NethermindEth/dotnet-libp2p).
- **Production implementation:** `src/Host.Windows/Networking/Libp2pPeerTransport.cs` implements `IPeerTransport`, pins the expected PeerId embedded in the multiaddress, verifies the authenticated remote PeerId reported by the established session, limits listening to an explicit address and requires a pinned identity for inbound sessions.
- **Application protocol:** `Libp2pPeerMessageProtocol.cs` sends/receives complete varint-prefixed binary messages, checks the configured size limit before transmission/allocation, applies bounded queue backpressure, and surfaces incomplete frames/remote disconnects as I/O errors. The transport uses the library's default TCP + Noise/TLS stack; no plaintext-forcing option is enabled.
- **Real integration test:** two actual libp2p peers ran in the HostLifecycle process. The final acceptance verified expected PeerId pinning, rejection of a mismatched identity before dialing, exact binary round-trip client→server and server→client, rejection of a payload over the configured 1 KiB bound, idempotent disconnect and immediate `IsConnected == false` after local disconnect.
